Our Heritage

A Story Told
One Rinse at a Time

In 2024, after a particularly long hike, our founder Vivo Raj found himself staring into a basin of post-walk foot wash water. The brownish hue. The earthy bouquet. The warmth.

He asked himself: why should this journey end in a drain? And with that existential pivot, AquaToes™ was born — the world's first luxury foot wash water brand.

"Our water doesn't just hydrate the body. It hydrates the soul — specifically, the part of the soul located between the toes."

Each bottle is sourced from a curated selection of feet, hand-selected for their terroir, arch complexity, and walking history. No two bottles are the same. No refunds either.
